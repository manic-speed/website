Why I recommend TCM Security

When people ask me they want to get into cybersecurity and where or how to start, I recommend TCM Security. I tell them that having gone through the materials and exams myself, the exam certifications are affordable, focus on real-world practicality, and have hands-on approach to learning. Best of all, there are no proctors or invasive monitoring software installed on your device when taking the exam. Every exam certification also includes a free retake voucher, giving you peace of mind. Combine all of that with their incredibly supportive Discord community, which I highly recommend you join, and it is easily one of the best investments you can make in your cybersecurity journey.

I took the Practical SOC Analyst Associate (PSAA) and passed it without too much struggle, mind you, I was already doing SOC related work. I have my eye on the Practical SOC Analyst Professional (PSAP) down the road. Explore the complete defensive catalog.
